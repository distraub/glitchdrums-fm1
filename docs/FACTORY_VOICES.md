# App factory voices on FM-1

F1 is INIT for every lane. F2–F8 follow the first seven named app presets.
INIT remains the startup sound and ORIGINAL kit. In VOICE PRESETS, hold ENV
and press SEL as an additional INIT shortcut. ARP compares it.

| Lane | F1 → F8 |
|---|---|
| kick | INIT · SUB FOUNDATION · DEEP HOUSE · TECH HOUSE · CLUB PUNCH · TRANCE KNOCK · 909 DRIVE · 808 BLOOM |
| snare | INIT · TIGHT WIRE · HARD CENTER · BIG ROOM · NOISE SLAP · RIM TIGHT · 909 CRACK · ELECTRO BODY |
| closedHat | INIT · TIGHT TICK · 909 CLOSED · DARK VELVET · BRIGHT SPRAY · ACID TICK · HOUSE SHUFFLE · TRAP PIN |
| openHat | INIT · 909 OPEN · HOUSE OFFBEAT · LONG AIR · DARK OPEN · BRIGHT WASH · SHORT OPEN · TRAP SIZZLE |
| clap | INIT · TIGHT CLAP · 909 CLAP · BIG HANDS · DRY SLAP · NOISE CLOUD · HOUSE STACK · DARK CROWD |
| percussion | INIT · WOOD BLOCK · COWBELL · CLAVE · FM BELL · METAL HIT · SHAKER · CONGA LOW |
| glitch | INIT · HELD RASP · FALLING REPEATS · SLOW 80 MS · SLOW 200 MS · FROZEN NOISE · PITCHED GROWL · SAW TEXTURE |
| analog | INIT · DFAM KICK · SUB PULSE · ACID TOM · METAL FM · NOISE HIT · LASER DROP · RESONANT PING |

Generated by compiling FactoryVoicePresetCatalog.swift, then selecting the
nearest existing INIT-calibrated control detents. Physical requested/encoded
values and source hashes are in FACTORY_VOICES.json. This is 7-bit parameter
quantization, not a bit-identical floating-point DSP port. PERC replaces TOM
in firmware 0.14.0. Low/High Tom do not have dedicated FM-1 lanes.

Voice recall keeps the arrangement and other lanes. It includes the source
preset level; firmware sound presets also reset their own pan/transpose/LFOs
to the INIT sound values. The eight user slots remain separate and writable.
